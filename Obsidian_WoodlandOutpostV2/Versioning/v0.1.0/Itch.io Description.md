### About
![[woodland_outpost_itch_cover_630x500 1.png]]
Winter is approaching!

Gather wood, stone, and food as you explore the forest. Develop your camp through several construction stages and complete the woodland cabin before time runs out.

### Features

- A compact survival and resource-management experience
- Procedurally generated woodland maps
- Gathering, building, and food-management systems
- Dynamic day-and-night atmosphere
- Original pixel-art environments and characters
- Saved audio and display settings
- Keyboard-friendly menus and controls

### Controls

- **WASD / Arrow Keys** — Move
- **E** — Gather or interact
- **Escape** — Pause or return
- **Enter** — Select menu option
- **Alt + Enter** — Toggle fullscreen

### Development status

🌱 **Woodland Outpost is still growing!**

This project is under heavy development, so you may encounter unfinished features, strange bugs, and workers occasionally making questionable life choices.

I’m learning how to improve and polish the game as I build it, and I’ll add more content as soon as it’s ready. Thank you for playing, and please feel free to share your feedback—it genuinely helps the outpost grow!
