# Woodland Outpost

**Explore the forest. Gather supplies. Build your shelter before winter arrives.**

Woodland Outpost is a compact 2D survival-management game made with Godot.

You arrive in the middle of a procedurally generated wilderness with only a small camp and a limited amount of time. Search the forest for wood, stone, and food, carry your supplies home, and gradually transform your camp into a cabin capable of surviving the winter.

## Plan every expedition

Resources are spread across a large forest, and not everything can be found close to camp.

Wood and food are available near the starting area, while valuable stone generally requires longer expeditions. An off-screen indicator will help you find your way home—but deciding when to return is up to you.

Your backpack can carry a maximum of **12 kg**, and every resource has a different weight:

- Food: 1 kg
- Wood: 2 kg
- Stone: 3 kg

Do you continue searching, or return to camp before your backpack becomes full?

## Gathering takes time

Gathering is not instantaneous.

Hold the interaction key while your character works, and watch the progress above each resource. Different materials require different amounts of time, and interrupted progress is preserved.

You can leave an unfinished resource, return later, and continue from where you stopped.

## Build before winter

Deposit your resources at camp and use them to unlock increasingly advanced construction stages.

The days continue to pass while you explore, and food is consumed at the end of each day. Complete the final cabin before winter arrives—or your woodland expedition will come to an unfortunate end.

## Features

- A procedurally generated forest.
- Distance-based resource distribution.
- Timed and resumable gathering.
- Limited backpack capacity.
- Resource-specific weights, sounds, and visual effects.
- Progressive camp construction.
- A complete day-and-night cycle.
- Navigation assistance for finding the camp.
- Contextual instructions for new players.
- Multiple paths through each expedition depending on what you gather first.

## Controls

| Action            | Keyboard           |
| ----------------- | ------------------ |
| Move              | WASD or arrow keys |
| Gather / interact | Hold E or ENTER    |
| Pause             | Esc                |
| Toggle fullscreen | Alt + Enter        |

## Development status

I know that Woodland Outpost is still a small and slightly silly game—but that is part of the fun!

This project is an experiment and, most importantly, my way of learning how to make games with Godot. I’m building it one mechanic at a time, testing new ideas, making mistakes, and gradually turning it into something more complete and enjoyable.

There is still plenty I would like to improve, so follow me on this little development journey!

If you play the game and have an idea, find a problem, or think something could be more fun, please let me know in the comments. Your suggestions could help shape the next version of Woodland Outpost!
